## Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Overview Dashboard** was built using transactional sales data from **Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, and South Africa**. The goal of the project was to transform raw business data into a clean, interactive, and decision-focused dashboard that supports KPI monitoring, trend analysis, regional comparison, customer segmentation, and product category insights.

This project was designed as a complete end-to-end business intelligence solution with a strong focus on:

- Data modeling  
- DAX calculations  
- Time intelligence  
- Executive dashboard design  
- Business storytelling  
- Interactive filtering experience  

---

## Business Problem

The raw dataset contained customer details, distributor information, product categories, sales representatives, and transactional sales records. While the data was rich, it did not provide business users with a clear understanding of:

- Revenue trends over time  
- Country-wise performance  
- Customer segment contribution  
- Product category dependency  
- Discount impact  
- Churn behavior  
- Target achievement  

The dashboard solves this problem by converting raw records into a structured and visually understandable business intelligence report.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project was to design a professional Power BI dashboard that provides an executive-level view of sales performance while allowing users to interact with the data and uncover deeper business insights.

## Data Modeling Approach

A proper **star schema** was created to ensure analytical accuracy and scalability. The data model connects the fact sales table with relevant dimension tables such as:

- Customers
- Products
- Distributors
- Sales Representatives
- Date Table

A separate **Date Table** was created and marked as a calendar table to support accurate time intelligence calculations such as monthly and yearly trend analysis.

## Dashboard Design Strategy

The dashboard was structured intentionally to support storytelling rather than simply displaying disconnected visuals.

The design flow follows this order:

1. Executive KPI overview  
2. Revenue trend analysis  
3. Country-level comparison  
4. Sales representative performance  
5. Customer segmentation  
6. Product category distribution  
7. Target monitoring and interaction support  

This approach improves clarity, readability, and decision-making value.
